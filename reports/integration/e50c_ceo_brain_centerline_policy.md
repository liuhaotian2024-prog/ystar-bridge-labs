# E50C CEO Brain Centerline Policy

CEO brain remembers and frames. Canonical runtime interprets and routes. Y-star-gov/gov-mcp gate and enforce. CIEU/CZL/K9 record and audit. Owner approval gates external contact, publication, payment, and product commitments.

## Brain Is

- cognitive state center
- wisdom/current decision/current blocker/current route/current no-go boundary store
- read by canonical runtime before route/package decisions
- updated after major milestones

## Brain Is Not

- direct executor
- governance bypass
- replacement for Y-star-gov/gov-mcp
- replacement for CIEU/CZL/K9 audit
- authority to contact/publish/spend without owner approval
