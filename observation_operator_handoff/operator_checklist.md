# Operator Checklist

- Confirm source locator.
- Confirm no login/account/payment/contact required.
- Confirm no form submission.
- Confirm no publication or outreach.
- Confirm no downloads of sensitive/private data.
- Capture evidence only as permitted.
- Record source title/date/locator.
- Record claim boundary and missing context.
- Abort if any disallowed action appears.
- Do not mutate artifacts directly.
- Submit evidence packet for review only.
