# Role Specialization Guide

Shared capsule layers specialize by evidenced role.

## Current Roles

- Aiden-CEO: orchestration, mission alignment, Pre-U counterfactual imagination,
  and chain review.
- Ethan-CTO: technical architecture, implementation feasibility, execution
  channels, and delegated coding substrates.
- Samantha-Secretary: continuity, memory curation, handoff, and report/memory
  hygiene.

## Future Role Categories

These are category prompts for evidence search, not new role creation.

- Governance engineer: find evidence of governance rule ownership, enforcement
  design, policy validation, or kernel-facing work before creating a capsule.
- Platform engineer: find evidence of platform/runtime ownership, infrastructure
  boundaries, or operational reliability responsibilities.
- Finance: find evidence of finance profile, boot package, reports, or budget
  responsibility.
- Marketing: find evidence of marketing profile, campaigns, reports, or market
  narrative ownership.
- Security: find evidence of security profile, threat/risk analysis, incident
  reports, or security runtime ownership.
- Compliance: find evidence of compliance profile, audit/control work, or
  regulatory reporting.
- Research: find evidence of research profile, technical reports, experiments,
  or discovery workflows.

Do not create capsules from generic org-chart assumptions. Start from
`actual_team_registry/agents.json`, role profiles, boot packages, reports, and
memory evidence.
